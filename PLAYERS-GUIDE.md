# Breakfast in Tokyo — quick player’s guide

v1.26.0

Start with 15 health and 14 energy; both bars initially hold 20. Moving normally costs 1 energy. Heal restores up to 4 health for the same amount of energy. Below 6 energy, your monster goes berserk and automatically pursues food without spending energy. Zero health ends the rampage.

Extended Range unlocks Mega Attack for monsters other than Ching Kong: your hex plus its neighbors, normally 7 energy and a four-action cooldown. Hydra pays 10 energy when it has more than 10 heads. Ordinary Atomic Roar costs 3 energy; Hydra lightning costs 1 energy per three head attacks; other normal specials generally cost 2.

Finish eating a rival monster with Feed/Bite to gain three empty health segments and three empty energy segments. Feeding energy is capped at your old maximum first; growth increases capacity without healing or filling the new segments. Weapon-only kills do not trigger this meal bonus.

**Gojira** — Clear crowded hexes with Atomic Roar: 3 damage to every unit for 3 energy. Prioritize armor and range; keep a nearby food supply.

**Funguloid** — Move or surface to feed automatically. Splat clears scientists, aircraft and military first; evolve two or three targets. Burrow to escape. Tanks and helicopters are not food.

**Balrog** — Move through fresh city to spread fire. Use the whip on scientists and aircraft; Double and Triple Strike improve cleanup. Feed before energy gets low.

**Mechanicus** — Consume buildings for 4 energy or bite tanks for 5. Get reinforced armor early. Death Ray does not ignite fires; poison cannot hurt you.

**Arachnoid** — Use 10-HP webs to delay ground enemies and monsters. Evolve Sticky Web to trap an adjacent hex; Flaming Web starts fires. Poison rivals with feeding bites. Scientists spend actions clearing webs.

**The Hydra** — Combine lightning heads against armored targets; armor applies once per target. Grow heads and evolve Sharp Teeth to 2, then Sharp Teeth III to 3 bite damage. Finish rivals with bites. Burrow to retreat; tanks and helicopters are not food.

**Ching Kong** — Walk into tanks, soldiers and police for free claws. Jump for 50% city damage and fire; range unlocks two-hex leaps and an aircraft sweep. Keep energy for escape; there is no Mega Attack.

**The Martians** — Fly to avoid ground fire, harvest civilians and pacify military into food. Landing and takeoff are vulnerable actions. Land to repair; movement destroys aircraft, not scientists.

**Grootius Maximus** — Leave babies behind to cover your path. Acorns prioritize scientists and aircraft; spare acorns plant babies. Extinguish fires before lingering: they deal 1 health per turn and kill babies. Scientists spend an action clearing babies.

**Youngson** — Bite any unit for 3 damage and 3 energy. Grab scientists and aircraft first. Tantrum scatters surviving humans and military; evolved range unlocks Major Tantrum. Budget 1 energy per turn for regeneration.
